Railway Stage 18 - quick setup

A. Public dashboard domain
1. Railway -> your project.
2. Click the python-telegram-bot service (do NOT click Postgres).
3. Open Settings.
4. Find Networking / Public Networking.
5. Click Generate Domain.
6. Wait for Railway to show the generated https domain.
7. Redeploy or Restart the bot once.

B. Variables (python-telegram-bot -> Variables)
ADMIN_USER_ID=<your numeric Telegram ID>
DASHBOARD_REFRESH_SECONDS=15
BETA_NOTICE_HOURS=12

You normally do NOT need to create RAILWAY_PUBLIC_DOMAIN yourself; Railway provides it after Generate Domain.
If needed, you may instead create:
DASHBOARD_BASE_URL=https://your-generated-domain

C. Delete a wrong coin safely
Send privately to the bot:
/deletecall PENIS
or preferably:
/deletecall <full CA>

Confirm with the red/green confirmation buttons. The row disappears from the database and live dashboard within seconds.
